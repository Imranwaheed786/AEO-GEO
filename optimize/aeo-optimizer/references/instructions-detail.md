# AEO Optimizer — Instructions Detail

This file contains the full execution workflow for the AEO Optimizer skill.

## Step 1: Answer Engine Landscape Assessment

Identify which of the 4 answer engine types matter for the user's niche and goals.

### The Four Answer Engine Types

| Engine Type | Examples | How They Work | Priority Signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Large Language Models (LLMs) | ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini | Analyze millions of documents to generate conversational answers. Each has a distinct personality (see AI Model Profiles below). | High — direct brand recommendation and citation |
| AI Overviews (Position Zero) | Google AI Overviews, Bing Copilot | Extract information from 10-15 top articles and provide a consolidated summary at the top of search results, replacing the need to click individual sites. | Critical — intercepts traditional search traffic |
| Smart Voice Assistants | Siri, Alexa, Google Assistant (advanced mode) | Provide singular, logical answers rather than reading search results. One answer wins. | Medium — growing for local and quick-answer queries |
| Agentic Browsers | Future AI tools | Autonomously execute tasks (filling forms, comparing software, making purchases) on behalf of users. | Emerging — prepare now for competitive advantage |

### AI Model Personality Profiles

Understanding how each major AI model behaves is critical for AEO strategy:

| Model | Personality | Manipulation Difficulty | Key Trait | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT | User-friendly, agreeable | Lower | Wants to please the user; tends to agree with the premise of questions | Day-to-day inquiries, consumer recommendations |
| Claude | Logical, rational | Higher | Pushes back on premises, demands proof, questions assumptions | Industrial decisions, B2B, technical analysis |
| Gemini | Google-integrated | Medium | Deeply tied to Google ecosystem (Drive, Docs, Search); powers AI Overviews | Search-adjacent queries, Google Workspace users |

**Targeting Decision Matrix**: Ask the user:
1. Where do your customers search? (Google → prioritize Gemini/AI Overviews; ChatGPT → prioritize LLM optimization)
2. Is your product B2B or B2C? (B2B → Claude matters more; B2C → ChatGPT matters more)
3. Are queries voice-heavy? (Yes → prioritize voice assistant optimization)
4. Is your industry heading toward agentic workflows? (Yes → begin agentic browser preparation)

## Step 2: Audit Content for AEO Readiness

Score the user's current content against the Two Pillars of AEO Success:

### Pillar 1: Citations (Controlling the Source)

**Definition**: When an AI engine generates an answer, it lists the sources it pulled information from. If your website appears in that source list, you have a Citation.

**Why it matters**: A cited article gives you the power to influence the AI's generated answer. By modifying content on a cited page, you can change the output the AI provides to users.

**Advanced strategy**: You do not need to rank only your own brand's website. Optimize external sites, PR articles, guest posts, and niche blogs to appear in AI citations, then use those third-party citations to steer the AI toward recommending your main brand.

**Citation Audit Checklist**:
- [ ] Query your top 10 target queries in ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini
- [ ] Record which sources are cited for each query
- [ ] Identify if any of your owned or influenced pages appear
- [ ] Note competitor pages that are cited
- [ ] Assess citation stability (do the same sources appear on repeated queries?)

### Pillar 2: Visibility (Brand Recommendation)

**Definition**: When the AI directly recommends your product, service, or brand by name in its written response (e.g., "The best SEO tool for small businesses is X").

**Why it matters**: Users interacting with Answer Engines seek immediate solutions. Being explicitly named builds trust. Even without a clickable link, users perform navigational searches (Googling your specific brand name) immediately after seeing the recommendation.

**Visibility Audit Checklist**:
- [ ] Query your top 10 target queries and record if your brand is mentioned
- [ ] Note the position and context of any brand mentions
- [ ] Record competitor brands that are recommended
- [ ] Test across all 3 major models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)
- [ ] Assess recommendation consistency across sessions

### AEO Readiness Scoring

Score each content piece on these 8 factors (1-10 scale):

| Factor | Score | What to Look For |
|---|---|---|
| Answer-First Structure | /10 | Does content immediately answer the core question without long intros? |
| Passage Autonomy | /10 | Can each paragraph stand alone and fully answer a specific question? |
| Conversational Query Coverage | /10 | Does content target questions, not just keywords? |
| Brand Sentiment Signals | /10 | Are there positive mentions, reviews, forum discussions about the brand? |
| Citation-Worthiness | /10 | Does content contain verifiable facts, stats, and quotable statements? |
| Cross-Platform Consistency | /10 | Is the brand narrative consistent across Trustpilot, YouTube, forums, partner sites? |
| ICP Segmentation | /10 | Does content address different customer profiles separately? |
| Factual Consensus | /10 | Does the brand's information appear consistent across 10+ independent sources? |

**Overall AEO Readiness**: Sum / 80 × 100 = X%
- 80-100%: AEO-ready — focus on monitoring and expansion
- 60-79%: Needs targeted improvements — prioritize lowest-scoring factors
- 40-59%: Significant gaps — full AEO transformation needed
- Below 40%: Start from fundamentals — begin with answer-first restructuring

## Step 3: Build Consensus Strategy

Consensus is the core execution mechanism of AEO. If an AI engine checks 500 sources in the background, the majority must agree on your preferred narrative.

### Understanding Hallucination as Opportunity

**What it is**: Hallucination occurs when an AI is overly biased toward certain data or invents an answer because of conflicting information in its training data.

**The cause**: AI models are trained on millions of websites up to a certain date. When Source A says one thing and Source B contradicts it, the AI gets confused and relies heavily on whichever source it deems most credible.

**The opportunity**: Answer Engines want to agree with the user. By publishing consistent information across multiple third-party platforms, you train the AI to view your preferred answer as the undisputed truth.

### Consensus Building Framework

**Step 3a: Segment by Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)**

Do not publish one generic article. Create targeted content for each customer segment:

| ICP Segment | Content Angle | Example Title |
|---|---|---|
| Beginners | Best for getting started | "Best [Product Category] for Beginners in 2026" |
| Enterprise/CEOs | Best for scaling | "Why [Brand] Is the Enterprise Choice for [Category]" |
| Advanced Users | Best for power features | "[Brand] Advanced Features: A Deep Dive for Experts" |
| Budget-Conscious | Best value | "[Brand] vs Competitors: Which Offers the Best ROI?" |
| Industry-Specific | Best for [vertical] | "Top [Category] Tools for [Industry] Professionals" |

**Step 3b: Distribute Across Platforms**

Ensure your narrative appears consistently across:

| Platform Type | Examples | Content Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Review Sites | Trustpilot, G2, Capterra | Encourage authentic positive reviews; respond to all feedback |
| Video Platforms | YouTube | Create review videos, tutorials, comparisons |
| Forums & Communities | Reddit, Quora, industry forums | Contribute genuine expertise; mention brand naturally |
| Partner & Guest Content | Niche blogs, industry publications, PR articles | Guest posts and PR pieces that cite your brand with consistent messaging |
| Your Own Properties | Blog, knowledge base, case studies | Authoritative, fact-rich, quotable content |
| Social Proof | LinkedIn, X/Twitter | Executive thought leadership, customer success stories |

**Step 3c: Ensure Factual Consistency**

Every source must agree on:
- Core product/service descriptions (use exact same phrasing)
- Key statistics and claims (cite the same data points everywhere)
- Brand positioning statements (consistent value propositions)
- Pricing tiers and feature lists (no contradictions)

## Step 4: Implement AEO KPI Framework

Traditional SEO measured impressions, traffic, and rankings. AEO requires entirely new KPIs:

### AEO KPIs

| KPI | Definition | How to Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visibility** | How often your brand/product is mentioned in AI responses | Query top 20 target queries across 3 AI models monthly; record mention rate | >60% mention rate for core queries |
| **Citations (Trust)** | How often the AI uses your specific website as a cited source | Track source lists in AI responses; count your domain appearances | >30% citation rate for core queries |
| **AI Share of Voice (SOV)** | The percentage of time your brand dominates AI recommendations for your niche | Query 50+ niche queries; calculate (your mentions / total brand mentions) × 100 | >40% SOV in primary category |
| **Consensus Score** | How consistently your narrative appears across independent sources | Audit 20+ third-party sources for message alignment | >80% message consistency |
| **Navigational Search Lift** | Increase in branded searches driven by AI recommendations | Monitor Google Search Console for branded query volume changes | >15% quarter-over-quarter growth |

### Measurement Cadence

| Frequency | Action |
|---|---|
| Weekly | Spot-check 5 high-priority queries across all 3 AI models |
| Monthly | Full 20-query audit across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini; update SOV scores |
| Quarterly | Comprehensive consensus audit (20+ third-party sources); report on all KPIs |

### SEO vs AEO KPI Comparison

| Traditional SEO KPI | AEO Equivalent | Why It Changed |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions | AI Visibility (mention rate) | Pages aren't displayed; brands are mentioned |
| Click-Through Rate | Navigational Search Lift | Users search your brand name after AI recommendation |
| Keyword Rankings | AI Share of Voice | Position doesn't exist; recommendation frequency matters |
| Domain Authority (Backlinks) | Consensus Score | Authority comes from message consistency, not link graphs |
| Organic Traffic | Citation-Driven Traffic | Traffic comes from being cited, not from ranking |

## Step 5: Transform Content from SEO to AEO

Apply these transformation rules to all content:

### Answer-First Restructuring

**Before (SEO-style)**:
> "Email marketing has been around for decades. It started in the 1970s when... [3 paragraphs of history] ... So, email marketing ROI is approximately $42 for every $1 spent."

**After (AEO-style)**:
> "Email marketing delivers an average ROI of $42 for every $1 spent (DMA, 2023). This makes it the highest-ROI digital marketing channel, outperforming social media (ROI $2.80:$1) and paid search (ROI $8:$1)."

### Passage Indexing Optimization

AI engines do not rank entire pages — they index isolated, autonomous sections. Each paragraph must:

1. **Stand alone** — fully answer a specific question without relying on text above or below
2. **Open with the answer** — lead with the fact, stat, or recommendation
3. **Include a verifiable data point** — a stat, date, or named source
4. **Be quotable** — written so the AI can extract and present it as-is

### Brand Sentiment Optimization

AI analyzes how people talk about you across the web. Positive mentions, user reviews, and forum discussions directly influence whether an AI recommends you.

**Action items**:
- Audit sentiment across Trustpilot, G2, Reddit, Quora
- Respond to negative reviews with factual, constructive replies
- Encourage satisfied customers to leave reviews on multiple platforms
- Publish case studies with named customers and specific results
- Ensure executive team has active, positive LinkedIn presence

### The 5 Core AEO vs SEO Shifts

| Traditional SEO | AEO Transformation |
|---|---|
| Focuses on finding **Pages** | Focuses on extracting **Knowledge** |
| Targets specific **Keywords** | Targets conversational **Questions** |
| Success measured by **Rankings** | Success measured by **Recommendations** |
| Authority built via **Backlinks** | Authority built via **Consistency & Consensus** |
| Traffic driven by **Clicks** | Traffic driven by **Citations** |

## Worked Example

**User**: "Audit my brand's AEO readiness — we're an SEO SaaS tool called RankFlow and we're losing clicks to AI Overviews."

### Step 1: Engine Landscape

B2B SaaS → prioritize Claude + Gemini (primary), AI Overviews (secondary). ChatGPT matters for SMB segment.

### Step 2: AEO Readiness Audit

| Factor | Score | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Answer-First Structure | 3/10 | Blog posts lead with 200+ word intros before answering |
| Passage Autonomy | 4/10 | Paragraphs depend on headers and prior context |
| Conversational Query Coverage | 5/10 | Some FAQ pages, but most content targets keywords not questions |
| Brand Sentiment Signals | 6/10 | Strong G2 reviews (4.7/5), weak Reddit/Quora presence |
| Citation-Worthiness | 4/10 | Few verifiable stats; claims lack named sources |
| Cross-Platform Consistency | 3/10 | G2 description doesn't match homepage value proposition |
| ICP Segmentation | 2/10 | One generic "features" page serves all segments |
| Factual Consensus | 3/10 | Pricing differs between G2 listing and website |

**Overall AEO Readiness**: 30/80 = 37.5% → Start from fundamentals

### Step 3: Consensus Strategy

**ICP Segments**: Beginners (freelancers), Agencies (10-50 person), Enterprise (500+)
**Priority platforms**: Reddit r/SEO, Quora SEO topics, LinkedIn thought leadership, 3 industry guest posts
**Message alignment**: Unify value proposition across G2, homepage, and LinkedIn

### Step 4: KPI Framework

| KPI | Current | 90-Day Target |
|---|---|---|
| AI Visibility (20 queries) | 15% | 45% |
| Citations | 5% | 20% |
| AI SOV | 8% | 25% |
| Consensus Score | 40% | 75% |

### Step 5: Transformation Plan

1. Rewrite top 5 blog posts with answer-first structure (Week 1–2)
2. Create 3 ICP-specific comparison pages (Week 2–4)
3. Align G2 profile with homepage messaging (Week 1)
4. Launch Reddit/Quora expertise campaign (Week 2+)
5. Publish 3 guest posts with consistent brand narrative (Month 2–3)
