# Consensus Building — AEO Execution Playbook

Consensus is the core execution mechanism of Answer Engine Optimization. This document provides the complete framework for building consensus across AI training sources.

## What Is Consensus?

When an AI engine generates an answer, it cross-references hundreds of sources in the background. If the majority of those sources agree on a specific narrative about your brand, the AI treats that narrative as established truth and presents it confidently to users.

**The goal**: Make your preferred brand narrative the dominant one across the web, so AI engines have no choice but to recommend you.

## Why Consensus Works: Understanding Hallucination

### The Mechanism

Hallucination occurs when an AI engine is overly biased toward certain data or invents an answer because of conflicting information in its training data.

**Example**: If Website A says a goldfish lives 8 years and Website B says 20 years, the AI gets confused. It resolves the conflict by relying heavily on whichever source it deems most "credible" — or worse, it invents a compromise answer.

### The Opportunity

Answer Engines are designed to be helpful and affirmative — they want to agree with the user. By publishing consistent information across multiple third-party platforms, you train the AI to view your preferred answer as the undisputed truth. When your narrative dominates, the AI "hallucinates" in your favor — not because it's wrong, but because the consensus of available information supports your position.

### Key Principle

You are not deceiving the AI. You are creating genuine, verifiable consensus. Every claim must be factually accurate — but the strategic advantage comes from ensuring that accurate information is present across many more sources than your competitors manage.

## The Consensus Building Framework

### Phase 1: ICP Content Segmentation

Do not publish one generic article about your brand. Create targeted content for each Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):

| ICP Segment | Content Angle | Example Title | Platform Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beginners | Best for getting started | "Best [Category] for Beginners in 2026" | Blog, YouTube, Reddit |
| Enterprise / CEOs | Best for scaling | "Why [Brand] Is the Enterprise Choice" | LinkedIn, G2, industry publications |
| Advanced Users | Best for power features | "[Brand] Advanced Features Deep Dive" | Technical blogs, Stack Overflow, documentation |
| Budget-Conscious | Best value / ROI | "[Brand] vs Competitors: ROI Comparison" | Review sites, comparison blogs |
| Industry-Specific | Best for [vertical] | "Top [Category] for [Industry]" | Industry publications, niche forums |

**Why segmentation matters**: When the AI encounters queries like "best X for beginners" or "best X for enterprise," it finds dedicated, authoritative content for each segment rather than one generic page trying to serve everyone.

### Phase 2: Multi-Platform Distribution

Ensure your narrative appears consistently across diverse platform types:

#### Tier 1: High-Authority Review Platforms
| Platform | Action | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Trustpilot | Encourage authentic reviews; respond to all feedback | Ongoing |
| G2 / Capterra | Maintain complete profile; encourage enterprise reviews | Monthly |
| Google Business Profile | Keep updated; respond to reviews | Weekly |

#### Tier 2: Content & Community Platforms
| Platform | Action | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube | Publish tutorials, reviews, comparisons | Bi-weekly |
| Reddit | Contribute genuine expertise; mention brand in relevant threads | Weekly |
| Quora | Answer industry questions authoritatively | Weekly |
| Industry Forums | Participate in discussions; share case studies | Ongoing |

#### Tier 3: Earned & Partner Media
| Platform | Action | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Guest Posts | Publish on niche blogs and industry publications | Monthly |
| PR Articles | Distribute newsworthy announcements | As available |
| Partner Sites | Co-create content with complementary brands | Quarterly |
| Podcast Appearances | Share expertise on industry podcasts | Monthly |

#### Tier 4: Owned Properties
| Platform | Action | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Company Blog | Authoritative, fact-rich, quotable articles | Weekly |
| Knowledge Base | Detailed how-to content, FAQs | Ongoing |
| Case Studies | Named customers, specific results, verifiable data | Monthly |
| LinkedIn (Executive) | Thought leadership, customer success stories | 2-3x weekly |

### Phase 3: Message Consistency Enforcement

Every source must align on these elements:

| Element | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Core Product Description | Use identical phrasing across all platforms | "[Brand] is a [category] platform that [core value proposition]" |
| Key Statistics | Cite the same verified data points everywhere | "Trusted by 10,000+ companies" — same number, same source |
| Brand Positioning | Consistent value propositions across all content | "The most [differentiator] solution for [audience]" |
| Pricing & Features | No contradictions between sources | Identical tier names, feature lists, pricing |
| Competitive Claims | Same comparisons, same data, same conclusions | Consistent win/loss framing vs. each competitor |

### Phase 4: Citation Strategy

**Direct citations**: Optimize your own website to appear in AI source lists.

**Indirect citations**: This is the advanced play. You do not need to rank only your own domain. Optimize external sites, PR articles, and niche blogs to appear in AI citations. Use those third-party citations to steer the AI toward recommending your brand.

**The indirect citation workflow**:
1. Identify which third-party sites are already cited by AI for your target queries
2. Create or pitch content to those sites that features your brand prominently
3. Ensure the content on those third-party sites uses your exact brand messaging
4. Monitor whether those third-party citations begin influencing AI recommendations toward your brand

### Consensus Score Calculation

```
Consensus Score = (Aligned Sources / Total Audited Sources) × 100
```

| Score Range | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 90-100% | Strong consensus | Monitor and maintain |
| 70-89% | Good consensus | Fix misaligned sources |
| 50-69% | Weak consensus | Prioritize content distribution |
| Below 50% | No consensus | Full consensus building campaign needed |
