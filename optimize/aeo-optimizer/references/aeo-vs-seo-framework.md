# AEO vs SEO — Transformation Framework

This reference document provides the complete framework for understanding and executing the shift from traditional SEO to Answer Engine Optimization (AEO).

## The Fundamental Shift

Search engines are evolving into "Answer Engines" by integrating Artificial Intelligence. Traditional search engines matched user queries to articles and provided links. Answer engines extract information from 10–15 top articles, read it, and provide a consolidated summary. Unlike traditional search, answer engines tailor their responses based on the user's profile and technical expertise.

**SEO is not dead — it has been demoted.** AEO is the necessary upgrade. Traditional SEO tactics (technical health, backlinks, content quality) remain the foundation, but they are no longer sufficient on their own.

## The 5 Core Differences

| Dimension | Traditional SEO | Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) |
|---|---|---|
| **Goal** | Focuses on finding **Pages** | Focuses on extracting **Knowledge** |
| **Targeting** | Targets specific **Keywords** | Targets conversational **Questions** |
| **Success Metric** | Measured by **Rankings** | Measured by **Recommendations** |
| **Authority Signal** | Built via **Backlinks** | Built via **Consistency & Consensus** |
| **Traffic Driver** | Driven by **Clicks** | Driven by **Citations** |

## The Two Pillars of AEO Success

### Pillar 1: Citations (Controlling the Source)

Citations occur when an AI engine lists your website as a source it pulled information from. Having a citation gives you the power to influence the AI's generated answer — by modifying content on your cited page, you can change what the AI tells users.

**Advanced play**: You can optimize external sites, PR articles, or niche blogs to appear in AI citations, then use those third-party citations to steer the AI toward recommending your brand.

### Pillar 2: Visibility (Brand Recommendation)

Visibility is when the AI directly names your product, service, or brand in its response. Users who see your brand recommended by an AI perform navigational searches (Googling your brand name) immediately after, even without a clickable link.

## KPI Transformation Map

| Traditional SEO KPI | AEO Equivalent | Why It Changed |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions | **AI Visibility** (mention rate) | Pages aren't displayed; brands are mentioned |
| Click-Through Rate | **Navigational Search Lift** | Users search your brand name after AI recommendation |
| Keyword Rankings | **AI Share of Voice** | Position doesn't exist; recommendation frequency matters |
| Domain Authority (Backlinks) | **Consensus Score** | Authority comes from message consistency, not link graphs |
| Organic Traffic | **Citation-Driven Traffic** | Traffic comes from being cited, not from ranking |
| Bounce Rate | **AI Recommendation Quality** | Clicks arrive pre-qualified by AI endorsement |

## Content Strategy Transformation Checklist

### From SEO-Style to AEO-Style Content

- [ ] **Answer First**: Eliminate long introductions. Lead every section with the direct answer.
- [ ] **Passage Autonomy**: Each paragraph must stand alone as a complete answer to a specific question, without relying on surrounding text.
- [ ] **Conversational Queries**: Target questions people ask ("How do I...", "What is the best...") rather than short keywords.
- [ ] **Quotable Statements**: Include specific statistics, named sources, and definitive claims the AI can extract verbatim.
- [ ] **Factual Density**: Replace vague claims ("many businesses") with verifiable data ("73% of B2B companies, per Forrester 2025").
- [ ] **Brand Sentiment**: Build positive brand mentions across review sites, forums, and social platforms.
- [ ] **Cross-Platform Consensus**: Ensure identical messaging across 10+ independent sources.
- [ ] **ICP Segmentation**: Create separate content for each customer segment rather than one generic piece.

### What to Keep from SEO

AEO does not replace SEO — it builds on top of it:

- [ ] **Technical health**: Site speed, mobile-friendliness, crawlability, HTTPS
- [ ] **Content quality**: Well-written, accurate, comprehensive coverage
- [ ] **Structured data**: Schema markup helps both search engines and AI understand content
- [ ] **Backlink profile**: Links still matter for ranking in traditional search, which feeds AI Overviews
- [ ] **On-page optimization**: Title tags, headers, meta descriptions remain relevant for AI Overview eligibility

### What to Add for AEO

- [ ] **Multi-platform presence**: Reviews, forums, guest posts, video, social proof
- [ ] **Consensus building**: Consistent brand narrative across all sources
- [ ] **AI-specific monitoring**: Regular querying of ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini for brand mentions
- [ ] **Citation tracking**: Monitoring which sources AI cites for your target queries
- [ ] **Model-specific optimization**: Different content emphasis for ChatGPT (agreeable) vs Claude (evidence-demanding) vs Gemini (Google-integrated)

## Decision Framework: When to Prioritize AEO Over SEO

| Scenario | Priority | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| Losing traffic to AI Overviews | AEO first | Your SEO rankings are being bypassed by AI summaries |
| Competitors appear in AI recommendations, you don't | AEO first | Consensus gap — competitors have stronger multi-platform presence |
| Low organic traffic baseline | SEO first | Need ranking foundation before AI can cite you |
| Strong rankings but declining CTR | AEO first | AI Overviews are intercepting your clicks |
| New brand / no web presence | Both simultaneously | Build SEO foundation and AEO consensus in parallel |
| High branded search volume | Monitor | You may already benefit from AI recommendations — verify and protect |
