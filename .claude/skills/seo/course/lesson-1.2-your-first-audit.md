# 1.2 Your First Audit
One command audits your entire site. Seven sub-agents run in parallel and deliver a scored report with a prioritized action plan.
The Command
/seo audit https://yoursite.com
Output Files
The audit produces two files in your project directory:
FULL-AUDIT-REPORT.md - Detailed findings from all 7 subagents
ACTION-PLAN.md - Prioritized fixes, sorted by impact
SEO Health Score (0-100
Run /seo audit on your site. What score did you get? Were there any surprises in the report?
