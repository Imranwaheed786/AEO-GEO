# 2.7 Schema Markup
Schema markup helps search engines and AI systems understand your content's meaning, not just its text. Proper schema leads to rich results in Google and 2.5x higher visibility in AI-generated answers.
Command:
/seo schema https://yoursite.com/page

Detection, Validation, and Generation
Claude handles schema in three steps:
Detection: Scans your page for existing JSON-LD, Microdata, and RDFa markup
Validation: Checks required and recommended properties against Google's schema specs
Generation: Creates ready-to-use JSON-LD for any missing schema types your page needs
AI visibility bonus: Pages with proper schema markup show 2.5x higher visibility in AI-generated answers. AI systems use schema to understand entities, relationships, and content structure with much higher confidence than plain text alone.
Run /seo schema on your most important page. Note which schema types are present and which are missing. Add the generated schema to your page.
