# 2.1 Technical Audit
9-Category Technical Audit
One command gives you a full technical SEO audit across 9 weighted categories. Each category is scored, prioritized, and rolled into an overall technical health score.
/seo technical https://yoursite.com
Claude crawls the page, inspects the HTML, headers, and metadata, then scores each of the 9 categories below.
Priority Classification
Every issue found gets a priority level so you know what to fix first:
Critical - Blocking indexing or causing major ranking loss
High - Significant impact on performance or visibility
Medium - Optimization opportunities with moderate impact
Low - Nice-to-have improvements
Fix Critical and High issues first. A single Critical issue (like a sitewide noindex tag) can tank your entire site.
Run /seo technical on your site right now. Identify your top 3 Critical or High priority issues and write them down. These are your first fixes.
