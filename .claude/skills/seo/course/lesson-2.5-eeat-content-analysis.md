# 2.5 EEAT Content Analysis
E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) is how Google evaluates content quality. Claude audits your pages against these four dimensions with weighted scoring.
Command: 
/seo content https://yoursite.com/page
E-E-A-T Scoring Weights
Trustworthiness is the highest weight because Google considers it the foundation. Without trust, the other three dimensions lose their value.
How Claude Audits E-E-A-T
Claude uses dimension-specific prompts to evaluate each page:
Experience: Does the content include personal anecdotes, screenshots, case studies, or original data?
Expertise: Is the content technically accurate and thorough? Does it go beyond surface-level information?
Authoritativeness: Is there an author bio? Are sources cited? Are there signals of brand authority?
Trustworthiness: Is there a privacy policy, contact info, editorial policy, and clear disclosures?
